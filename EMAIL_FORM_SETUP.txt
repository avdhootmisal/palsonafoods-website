PALSONA FOODS Website Enquiry Form Setup

The Contact Us form now sends enquiries to:
palsonafoods@gmail.com

Subject format:
WEBSITE_ENQUIRY_<NAME>_<CITY>

IMPORTANT - one-time FormSubmit activation:
1. Deploy the updated files to your GitHub Pages website.
2. Submit one test enquiry from the live website.
3. FormSubmit will send an activation email to palsonafoods@gmail.com.
4. Open that email and click the activation/confirmation link.
5. After activation, future enquiries will be delivered automatically.

No backend server is required. This works with the existing GitHub Pages static website.

The email form service used is FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
